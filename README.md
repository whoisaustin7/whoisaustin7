name: "Austin Melroy Dsouza"

I'm an **Audit Assistant at Gonsalves & Nayak**, where I handle statutory audits, GST/TDS compliance, income tax filing, and regulatory documentation across a wide portfolio of client entities. My work sits at the intersection of **financial compliance** and **data analytics** — I don't just process numbers, I build the dashboards and reporting systems that make them usable.

Before this, I spent several years at **Waffee House**, starting as an Operations Associate and being promoted to **Senior Operations Manager**, where I drove measurable operational improvements across sales, footfall, and waste reduction in a fast-paced F&B environment.

I hold a **Bachelor of Commerce from St. Aloysius Degree College, Karnataka**, and I've built a self-directed technical layer on top of my finance background — Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and DAX — to turn audit and business data into clear, actionable dashboards. I'm currently expanding into **HR & people analytics**, backed by a self-built HR Analytics Dashboard, an HR-focused certificate from GE Aerospace, and a Strategy Consulting certificate from Accenture.
